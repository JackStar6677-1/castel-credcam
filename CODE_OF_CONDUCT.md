# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct & Sovereign Moderation Standards

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders of the **castel-credcam** project pledge to make participation in our community, issues, and pull requests a harassment-free, respectful, and technically rigorous experience for everyone.

---

## 🛡️ Zero Tolerance Policy on Harassment, Toxicity & Abuse

We maintain an **absolute zero-tolerance policy** towards disrespect, personal attacks, and disruptive behavior:

### Unacceptable Behavior Includes:
* **Direct insults, ad-hominem attacks, name-calling, or derogatory language.**
* **Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, personal or political attacks.**
* **Toxic entitlement:** demanding timelines, insulting project architecture without technical justification, or harassing maintainers to accept unsolicited pull requests.
* **Public or private harassment in issues, discussions, or pull requests.**
* **Publishing others' private information (doxxing)** without permission.
* **Bad-faith engagement:** opening inflammatory issues, flooding threads with spam or automated low-quality AI comments, or evading prior blocks.

---

## ⚖️ Enforcement & Moderation Protocol

Project maintainers are empowered and obligated to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and pull requests that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

When unacceptable behavior is observed, maintainers will enforce the following actions immediately:

1. **Correction / Warning**: Hiding the offensive comment as *abusive* or *off-topic* and issuing a formal notice.
2. **Conversation Lock**: Immediate locking (`Lock conversation as spam / abusive / heated`) of the issue or pull request.
3. **Immediate Account Ban / Block**: Immediate block of the offending GitHub account from all repositories under this organization/user without debate.
4. **Escalation**: Serious threats, harassment, or Terms of Service violations will be escalated directly to **GitHub Trust & Safety**.
